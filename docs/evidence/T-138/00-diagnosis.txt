## Cause
oif's HA values (T-124) run three receivers with replicationFactor 3. The Bitnami Thanos chart 16.0.7 that Run:ai uses
lists the receivers in their hashring only with receive.service.additionalHeadless=true; without it the ring is
127.0.0.1 alone (templates/_helpers.tpl "thanos.receive.config") and a write that needs 2 of 3 replicas fails.
Every metric the Overview and Analytics pages read was missing on clusters with three control-plane nodes since T-124.
Single-node and two-node installs (replicationFactor 1) are not affected. Not a Run:ai defect: its defaults are one
receiver.
